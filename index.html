Understanding Compressed Biogas (CBG)

An Advanced Drop-In Replacement for Natural Gas

CBG is a highly purified, methane-rich biofuel produced through the anaerobic digestion of organic materials, including agricultural residues and cattle waste. By stripping out trace carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) and hydrogen sulfide ($H_2S$), the gas is enriched to match properties equal to fossil-derived natural gas, containing over 98% methane purity.

Automotive Fueling Integration

Acts as a direct drop-in replacement for conventional CNG, refueling over 32 lakh gas-based commercial and private vehicles via standard Oil Marketing Company (OMC) retail networks.

Industrial & Commercial Utility

Optimally suited for direct deployment within industrial combustion boilers, large-scale commercial cooking setups, and decentralized clean electricity generation modules.

Next-Gen Bio-Refining

High-Solids Dry Digestion Technology

VSDRP Green Energy utilizes advanced **Dry Anaerobic Digestion** architecture, an industrial upgrade specifically engineered to process stackable, high-fibrous organic feedstock without massive liquid dilution.

Breaking the Constraints of Traditional Wet Digestion

Unlike older standard wet methanation setups that require diluting input material into a thin liquid slurry (under 10% solids), our **Dry Technology** processes high-density biomass containing **20% to 40% total solids (TS)**.

This allows the plant to directly accept heavy agricultural materials like chopped Super Napier grass, straw matrices, and dense maize silages directly into the system, drastically minimizing raw water consumption and eliminating process optimization bottle-necks.

Dry Technology Structural Superiority

  • ✓ 90% Less Fresh Water Input: Minimizes water dilution requirements.
  • ✓ No Scum Layers: Continuous structural mechanical mixing prevents crusting.
  • ✓ Compact Footprint: Yields far higher biogas volume per cubic meter.
  • ✓ Concentrated FOM Manure: Requires zero extensive dewatering to recycle back to farms.

Integrated 12 TPD Dry-Processing Lifecycle

01

High-Solids Feeding

Harvested Super Napier grass and silages are precisely shredded and pushed via heavy-duty shaft augers directly into the digesters.

02

Dry Fermentation

The high-density material undergoes continuous biological transformation under thermophilic control regimes, maximizing raw carbon separation.

03

Refining Array

The raw gas is transferred into chemical scrubbers that strip out $CO_2$ and $H_2S$, compressing methane purity above 98%.
